# Retro Recoil - implementation meta

Non-code text and reference notes live here instead of inside the Python, so
the in-game Controls screen and anything else that quotes static text reads
it from this file at runtime rather than from a hardcoded string.

## controls

The Controls screen (`rr_screens.py`, `page_controls`) reads three blocks from this file, one
page each.  `CONTROLS` and `HUD` lines are `LABEL | meaning`; `HOWTO` lines are plain text,
`- ` starts a bullet.  Keep the start/end markers.  Edit the text here, no code change needed.

<!-- CONTROLS START -->
W/UP | Walk forward (hold it)
S/DOWN | Walk backward
A | Shuffle left
D | Shuffle right
LEFT/RIGHT | Turn left / right
E | Shoot
Q | Reload
C | Crouch / stand up
SPACE | Stop moving at once
ESC | Leave the match (asks first)
<!-- CONTROLS END -->

<!-- HUD START -->
K-n | Your kills this match
D-n | Your deaths this match
A-n | Rounds left in the gun (15 per magazine)
orange n.n | Seconds until you can shoot again (shown only when the gun is empty)
a>b | Kill feed, top right: a shot b
red ! | An enemy off screen can shoot you.  Its spot shows where: bottom middle = behind you, left/right = on that side; it slides to the edge as they come round and goes once they are on screen.  Two stacked = close.  One per enemy.
+ | Crosshair: aim with this, then press E
m:ss | Time left in the match (turns red in the last 15 s)
YOU DIED | You are out until the respawn countdown ends
<!-- HUD END -->

<!-- HOWTO START -->
- Goal: get as many kills as you can with as few deaths as possible.  The ranking is KDR (kills divided by deaths), then kills.
- A match lasts 5 minutes.  You cannot be hurt for the first 5 s of a match or for 3 s after you respawn (about 5 s after dying).
- Aim: turn with LEFT/RIGHT until the enemy is on the + in the middle, then press E.  Shots land instantly and are a little forgiving around the crosshair.  One round per press.
- Ammo: 15 rounds.  Q reloads in 1.5 s, but only 3 s after your last shot.  An empty gun means waiting for that countdown.
- Cover: walls block shots, and so do crates.  Crouch (C) to slip through low holes and hide pockets.  A crouched player can only be hit from within 9 m, unless you crouch too.
- Moving: hold W or UP.  A tap is a short step.  Over telnet the game only knows a key is held while it keeps repeating, so after you let go you coast for a moment.  SPACE stops you at once.
- Watch the red ! marks: bots shoot from behind too.
- Modes: Bot Practice is you against 5 bots.  Multiplayer fills a public lobby and starts after a short wait.  Private Team gives you a 5-character code for friends to join.
<!-- HOWTO END -->

## map-format (retro_recoil_map.obj)

Units are metres. +x is east, +z is north, y is up. Loaded by `rr_world.py`
(`WorldMap`), generated by `make_map.py`.

* A `v`/`vn` pair forming a **vertical** quad (`f`) is a wall piece. The
  `vn` normal is the outward-facing side; `usemtl` names the material used
  for rendering/collision height and must be one of the keys in
  `rr_world.MATERIALS` (`wall`, `pillar`, `lintel`, `sill`, `hide`,
  `cover`). The quad's own min/max `y` is its vertical extent, so a
  doorway is simply the wall segment *above and beside* the opening - there
  is no separate "opening" object.
* A **horizontal** quad (`f` where every vertex shares one `y`) is a floor
  or ceiling plane, grouped (`g`) as:
  - `yardA` / `yardB` - the two courtyard ground planes (walkable, open sky)
  - `roof` - ceiling over the hallway ring (blocks sky, normal headroom)
  - `lowceil` - ceiling over a crouch hole / hide pocket, fixed at
    `LOWCEIL_H` (1.0m) regardless of the quad's own `y` - anything under
    one of these can only be entered crouched
* A `p` (point) element is a marker, grouped as `spawnA`, `spawnB` (spawn
  points for that yard) or `hide` (one point per hide pocket, used for bot
  AI roaming/ambush spots, not a spawn point).
* 16 wall openings per yard (an opening is a gap left in a `wall_run`):
  8 normal doorways (full headroom) and 4 crouch holes (`HOLE_H` tall,
  behind which is an isolated dead-end pocket with a 90 degree turn), for
  32 total across the two yards. The yards share one "main hallway"
  (built by `build_main_hallway`) with a crouch-height vent in each side's
  wall - the only line of sight between the two yards outside of actually
  walking the hallway.

## account / stats

`online.db` (sqlite3, see `rr_db.py`) holds `accounts` (username, salted
pbkdf2 password hash) and `stats` (lifetime kills/deaths/reloads/games and
the deaths of the last 5 matches). KDR is always `kills / max(1, deaths)`,
computed on read - never stored, so it can't drift from kills/deaths.

## intro animation

If `start.gif` exists in the server's working directory at startup, it is
converted once into `ascii.bin` (80x40 base grid) and `petscii.bin` (40x25,
the native Commodore screen size) - see `rr_intro.py`. Either file is
rebuilt automatically if it goes missing while the gif is still there; if
the gif itself is missing, no intro is converted or shown. Playback happens
once, right after a successful login or account creation, holds the last
frame for 2 seconds, then proceeds to the main menu.
